## Article 1 — Our Return Policy

**Category:** Returns
**Title:** Our Return Policy

Cascade Outdoor Supply accepts returns within **60 days** of the delivery
date on most items.

To be eligible for a return, gear must be unused, in its original packaging,
and accompanied by proof of purchase. Items showing signs of field use are
handled under our repair and warranty process instead.

A few categories are not returnable:

- Climbing hardware, harnesses, ropes, and any load-bearing safety equipment
- Items marked Final Sale at the time of purchase
- Gift cards
- Nutrition, fuel canisters, and other consumables

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Once we receive and
inspect your return, expect the refund to appear within 5–7 business days.

Return shipping is free for orders delivered within the United States. For
international orders, return shipping is the customer's responsibility.

If your item arrived damaged or defective, don't use the standard return
process — see *Damaged or Defective Items on Arrival*.

## Article 2 — How to Track Your Order

**Category:** Orders and Shipping
**Title:** How to Track Your Order

Every Cascade order gets a tracking number as soon as it leaves our
warehouse. You'll receive it by email at the address on the order.

You can also find tracking on your order in your Cascade account, under
Order History.

Tracking usually takes **up to 24 hours** to start showing movement after
you receive the number. If tracking hasn't updated after two business days,
the package may be delayed in transit.

Orders containing multiple items may ship in more than one box, each with its
own tracking number. Your order isn't incomplete just because one box arrived
first.

If tracking shows your package as delivered but you don't have it, check with
neighbors and building management first, then contact us — we'll open a case
and work it with the carrier.

## Article 3 — Shipping Options and Delivery Times

**Category:** Orders and Shipping
**Title:** Shipping Options and Delivery Times

Delivery times are counted in business days from when your order ships, not
from when you place it. Orders placed before 1:00 PM Pacific on a business
day ship the same day.

| Option | Delivery Time | Cost |
|--------|---------------|------|
| Standard | 5–7 business days | Free over $75, otherwise $8.95 |
| Expedited | 2–3 business days | $16.95 |
| Overnight | Next business day | $34.95 |

Overnight orders must be placed before 11:00 AM Pacific to ship the same day.

We ship to all 50 U.S. states. Alaska and Hawaii are Standard only, and
delivery typically runs 7–10 business days.

International shipping is available to Canada and Mexico. Duties and import
taxes are the customer's responsibility and are collected by the carrier on
delivery, not by Cascade.

Oversized items — kayaks, canoes, and rooftop cargo boxes — ship freight and
are quoted separately at checkout.

## Article 4 — Exchanges and Finding the Right Size

**Category:** Returns
**Title:** Exchanges and Finding the Right Size

We don't process direct exchanges. To swap an item for a different size or
color, return the original for a refund and place a new order. That way you
don't wait on the return to clear before your replacement ships.

Sizing runs true to the manufacturer's chart, and those charts vary between
brands. Each product page links the sizing chart for that specific brand.

For boots, we recommend measuring your foot late in the day and sizing up a
half size if you plan to wear thick hiking socks.

For layering pieces, check whether the item is cut as a base layer, mid
layer, or shell — base layers are intended to fit close, and a base layer
that feels snug is usually fitting correctly.

If you're between sizes and unsure, log a case and our support team will help
you choose before you order.

## Article 5 — Damaged or Defective Items on Arrival

**Category:** Orders and Shipping
**Title:** Damaged or Defective Items on Arrival

If your gear arrives damaged, or has a manufacturing defect, don't send it
back through the standard return process.

Contact us within **14 days** of delivery and we'll handle it directly. Have
ready:

- Your order number
- A short description of the problem
- Photos of the damage, including the shipping box if the box was damaged too

We'll ship a replacement at no cost, or issue a full refund including any
shipping you paid, whichever you prefer.

Damage from field use — abrasion, punctures, wear on seams — is not a defect
on arrival, and is handled through our repair and warranty process instead.

Keep the packaging until the claim is resolved. Carriers sometimes require
inspection.

## Article 6 — Caring for Your Tent

**Category:** Product Care
**Title:** Caring for Your Tent

A tent that's stored wet will not last. Everything else is secondary to that.

**After every trip.** Set the tent up or hang it until it's fully dry, inside
and out, before packing it away. Shake out grit — sand and pine needles abrade
the floor coating faster than anything else.

**Cleaning.** Sponge with cool water and a non-detergent soap. Never machine
wash, never put a tent in a dryer, and don't use bleach or fabric softener —
all of them strip the waterproof coating.

**Zippers.** Rinse grit out of zipper teeth and run a dry lubricant along them
once a season. A blown zipper is the most common tent repair we see, and it's
almost always grit.

**Storage.** Store loosely in a breathable bag, not compressed in the stuff
sack. Long-term compression sets creases that become leak points.

**Re-proofing.** If water stops beading on the fly and starts soaking in, the
DWR finish has worn through. A spray-on re-proofer restores it. That's normal
wear, not a defect.

## Article 7 — Caring for Down and Synthetic Insulation

**Category:** Product Care
**Title:** Caring for Down and Synthetic Insulation

Insulation works by trapping air. Anything that mats the fill flat — dirt,
body oils, compression — reduces warmth.

**Storage.** Store sleeping bags and puffy jackets uncompressed. Most bags
ship with a large mesh storage sack for exactly this. Leaving a bag in its
stuff sack between trips permanently reduces its loft.

**Washing down.** Use a down-specific wash, front-loading machine only, on a
gentle cycle. Tumble dry on low with two or three clean tennis balls to break
up clumps. It takes several hours, and stopping early leaves damp clumps
inside that will mildew.

**Washing synthetic.** Mild detergent, gentle cycle, tumble dry low. Synthetic
is more forgiving than down but the same rule applies — dry it completely.

**Spot cleaning.** For collar and cuff grime, a little diluted soap on a cloth
is better than washing the whole garment. Every wash costs a little loft.

**When loft doesn't come back.** If a bag stays flat after a proper wash and
dry, the fill may be past its life. Log a case with photos and we'll take a
look.

## Article 8 — Checking the Status of Your Support Case

**Category:** Support
**Title:** Checking the Status of Your Support Case

When you contact Cascade support, we open a case and send you a case number
by email. Keep that number — it's the fastest way for us to find your issue.

You can check your case status by giving your case number to our support
assistant in chat, or by signing in to your Cascade account and opening
Support History.

Cases move through these statuses:

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| New | Received, not yet picked up |
| In Progress | A support agent is actively working it |
| Waiting on Customer | We need information from you before we can continue |
| Escalated | Routed to a specialist team |
| Closed | Resolved |

If your case shows **Waiting on Customer**, check your email — we've asked you
a question and the case won't move until you reply.

Our first response target is one business day. Cases opened over a weekend
are typically picked up the next business day.
